First author paper on synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale for learning logical reasoning and beyond.

**Abstract**: SynLogic presents a method for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale to train large language models in logical reasoning tasks. The approach generates structured reasoning data that can be used for supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning.
