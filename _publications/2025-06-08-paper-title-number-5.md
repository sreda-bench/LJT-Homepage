**SynLogic: Synthesizing Verifiable Reasoning Data at Scale for Learning Logical Reasoning and Beyond**

*Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, Junxian He*

**Abstract**: SynLogic is a novel framework for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale to enable learning of logical reasoning capabilities. The approach addresses key challenges in training LLMs for complex reasoning tasks by generating high-quality, verifiable reasoning traces.

**Key Contributions**:
- A scalable data synthesis pipeline for logical reasoning
- Verifiable reasoning trace generation
- Extensive evaluation across multiple reasoning benchmarks
