First author paper on investigating perception bottlenecks in Vision-Language Models for chart understanding tasks.

**Abstract**: This work explores the fundamental limitations of current Vision-Language Models (VLMs) when processing charts and visualizations, identifying key perception bottlenecks that hinder accurate understanding.
